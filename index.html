The league

La Liga is the top division of Spanish football. Twenty clubs play each other home and away, and the club with the most points at the end of the season wins the title.

Beyond the Madrid and Barcelona giants, clubs like Athletic Club, Sevilla and Villarreal keep the title race unpredictable, and El Clásico remains one of the biggest games in world sport.
